Project Context Notes

Budget Buddy helps you manually track income, expenses, and savings goals in one private, offline app.

You can capture recurring charges like rent, subscriptions, and utility bills so your monthly plan is visible before money leaves your account.

Many users want to monitor category-level spending such as groceries, dining, transport, clothing, and entertainment.

Recording each expense with category and amount supports better awareness than checking a statement at the end of the month.

This prototype demonstrates how a user could compare expected vs. actual spending and spot trends.

College students may track irregular income from part-time work, stipends, gifts, and side gigs.

Early-career professionals may use the app to set weekly limits while building emergency savings.

Working parents may prioritize household categories and upcoming due dates for family expenses.

The app avoids bank linking and keeps all information on the local device to reduce privacy concerns.

To keep data entry lightweight, users can add short notes and use familiar categories.

Weekly review views can show where overspending happened and where goals were met.

Budget goals can be simple: spend less than a target in one category, or save toward a specific purchase.

Over time, users can adjust goals based on real habits instead of unrealistic starting plans.

This course prototype focuses on clear interaction design and does not require servers, external APIs, or account setup.

In later phases, this foundation can expand with richer flows for recurring entries, summaries, and progress feedback.
